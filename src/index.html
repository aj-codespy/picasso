How the collection grows

No. 1

Drop a screenshot

Any interface screenshot, from any folder — picasso setup points the gallery at where your shots live.

No. 2

Vision AI reads it

One command — picasso update — sends your screenshots to your chosen vision model (OpenAI, Google, NVIDIA NIM, or OpenRouter), which writes the description, tags, components, palette, and ideas.

No. 3

It joins the wall

The work is analysed and appears in the gallery instantly. Offline, by double-click — no server, no cloud. Open it with picasso inspire.
